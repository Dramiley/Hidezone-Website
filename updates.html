3.1.0Latest Nov 2026

Capture the Flag, secret roles, vague pings, Auto-Tag and Korean.

New

  • Capture the Flag mode. Two teams, one flag each on their own half of the arena. Teams place their flag on the map during the prepare time (or it spawns at a random spot). Walk up to the enemy flag to grab it and bring it home to score. Everyone can be tagged on the other team's half, the flag carrier everywhere. Tagged players walk back to their own flag to rejoin. First team to the capture target wins; on a tie when time runs out, the Golden Flag decides: the next capture wins.
  • Vague pings (free, every ping mode). Pings can show a circle the player is somewhere inside instead of the exact spot, so good hiding spots matter again. Players who stay put keep the same circle, so it cannot be narrowed down by comparing pings.
  • Auto-Tag as a third tag type next to Distance and PIN. Tag automatically when you get close enough (adjustable from 3 to 30 m), starting after the hiding phase.
  • Secret roles (Pro) for Hide & Seek, Zombie, Freeze Tag and Mr. X. Nobody knows their role at the start: everyone scatters during the hiding phase, then the roles are revealed and the hunt begins. The lobby shows one shared list.
  • Korean is the tenth app language.
  • Level rewards overview: tap "Next reward" in your stats to see every reward.
  • Restore purchases in Settings, for using Pro on a second phone with the same store account.
  • New lobbies now start with the settings of your last game.

Changes

  • Chase mode retired. Vague pings now bring its idea to every mode. You can rebuild it in Hide & Seek with vague pings and a short ping interval.
  • Less battery use: the map only redraws when you actually move, and the game screen no longer refreshes every second while you stand still.
  • Smoother chat, especially while the map is open behind it.
  • Zombie with Auto-Tag: holding the cure heals the nearest zombie automatically, at twice the Auto-Tag range.
  • Two seekers tagging the same hider at once now count as one catch.
  • Capture the Flag: a flag placed in the last second of setup is no longer moved to a random spot.
  • Every lobby sends less data per update.
3.0.1 Nov 9, 2026

Never get eliminated by the zone without noticing again.

New

  • Out-of-zone alert: your phone vibrates and shows a large warning with red screen edges as soon as you leave the zone, repeating while you stay outside. A notification with strong vibration also reaches you when the screen is locked. Can be switched off in your settings.
  • Post-game map: see where everyone was when the game ended, with your own position live, so the group finds each other again. The host can set a meeting point, and everyone sees the distance to it.
  • Feet and miles: a Metric / Imperial switch. US players start on imperial automatically. Zone sizes, distances, help texts and stats follow your choice.
  • Waiting seekers now see whether the game uses PIN tagging instead of a catch distance.

Changes

  • No more "Allow all the time" location permission. Tracking still continues while the screen is locked during a match.
  • A distinct message when you are eliminated for staying outside the zone.

Fixes

  • The zone transition duration no longer jumps back to 20 seconds when reopening the settings.
  • Countdowns stay visible in languages with long labels (for example French).
  • Cleaner Android notification icon and more stable startup on devices without the consent plugin.
3.0.0 Nov 1, 2026

Bomb Tag, custom play areas and a new economy.

New

  • Bomb Tag mode: one player holds a ticking bomb and must tag someone to pass it on. When the fuse runs out, the holder is out; the last player standing wins. Options for fuse length, a no-return lock, a hidden fuse (Pro) and automatic passing by proximity (Pro).
  • Custom play areas: draw the playground on the map with up to 12 corners, for example to leave out roads or water. Each shrink stage is a smaller copy of your shape. Saved in presets and replays.
  • Item selection: switch individual items off for your group (free).
  • Fixed cash economy: everyone starts with the same credits and never gets more (free; changing the amount is Pro).
  • Hidden-player notice: after a ping you see how many players a Ghost or Camouflage kept off the map, so a missing marker no longer looks like a bug.
  • Turkish is now a full app language.

Balance

  • Seekers now earn credits per tag.
  • Zone and circle sizes are shown as diameters everywhere.
  • The smallest custom area is 5,000 m².

Fixes

  • Zombie distance tags and cures are now processed atomically, so two players can no longer act on the same target at once.
  • Shops and target lists close when your role changes (for example after being infected).
  • Replay videos frame the current zone, and many smaller stability fixes.
